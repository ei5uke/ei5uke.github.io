  • Price: ¥1200
  • Rating: 8.5/10
  • Last date I had it : 2025-01-08
  • Review: classic bowl with seafood broth and a soy sauce base. everything is really clean and refreshing, and the wontons add a fresh twist. for me, the star is the chicken chashu which has a clean fattyness w/ a slight spice aftertaste that shines through the rest of the bowl. overall, a great classic example of chuuka soba w/ a seafood broth.
